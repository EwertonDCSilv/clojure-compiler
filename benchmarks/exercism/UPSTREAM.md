## Local changes

The upstream exercise implementations are preserved as the basis of the seven promoted
fixtures. This repository adds only the benchmark integration around them: deterministic
input generation, a `benchmark` function, a `-main` checksum entry point, runners,
reports and performance analysis.

The compatibility matrices are generated by compiling files from a separate checkout;
they do not copy all 493 upstream files into this repository.

This suite is an independent compiler-engineering corpus. It is not an official
Exercism benchmark, and the results do not imply endorsement by or affiliation with
Exercism.
